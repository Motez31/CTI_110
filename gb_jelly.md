# Teach a robot to make PB & J Sandwich

## Assumptions
- We have a fully charged humanoid robot 
- We have a countertop with the following items:
    - butter knife
    - jar of PB
    - jar of jelly
    - 2 slices of bread laid side by side on a plate


## Steps to Follow
1. posotion self in front of items
2. open the PB
3. open the jelly
4. grab the knife
5. use the knife to scoop out some PB
6. pick up the bread and Spread PB over flat surface of the bread
7. put PB bread back onto plate
8. use the knife to scoop out some Jelly
9. pick up the bread and Spread Jelly over flat surface of the bread
10. put down the knife 
11. place jelly bread onto PB bread with covered surfaces together
12. cut the sandwich in half with a knife
13. replaace the lids on the jar
14. put jars amd knife away


## Done When 
- Sandwich is contstructed and cut in half
- Area must be clear except for plate and sandwich
